Map help

Understand the Land Governance map themes, individual datasets, crop-year comparison, geographic coverage, and important research limitations.

How to use the map

1. Choose an areaSelect Ontario, Alberta, or Saskatchewan from the control at the top.
2. Turn layers onUse theme switches for groups or use individual switches to refine the map.
3. Compare crop yearsUse the Map Layers switches to select the 2011 or 2024 AAFC Crop Inventory.
4. Inspect featuresSelect a mapped boundary or polygon to view its available attributes and context.

Theme switches

Agricultural change

Controls the 2011 and 2024 AAFC crop inventories, agricultural capability, and national administrative boundaries.

Land governance

Controls municipal or township boundaries, available parcel references, Crown/public-land records, and national administrative boundaries.

Indigenous data · paused

No Indigenous boundary layer is published pending governance direction from Chiefs of Ontario and participating Nations.

Data access

Displays available boundary and parcel-reference coverage. It helps reveal uneven data access but does not contain complete ownership or transaction records.

Dataset layers

Province & territory boundaries Canada-wide

Statistics Canada 2021 boundaries for all 10 provinces and 3 territories. These provide national geographic context.

Agricultural capability · 1:1M Generalized

Canada Land Inventory classifications representing agricultural potential and soil limitations. The 1:1,000,000 scale is suitable for regional context, not parcel-level decisions.

Municipal & township boundaries

Ontario municipal boundaries, Alberta Township System outlines, or Saskatchewan rural-municipality boundaries. The geographic unit differs by province.

Parcel & lot references Partial coverage

Niagara Falls parcel polygons in Ontario, an Edmonton assessment-centroid display sample in Alberta, and Saskatchewan township references. These are not equivalent province-wide legal parcel datasets.

Crown & public land Province-specific

Selected Ontario and Saskatchewan public-land records. Availability, definitions, and attributes differ between provinces.

Indigenous and treaty data Paused

Federal administrative derivatives are not displayed. Chiefs of Ontario and participating Nations will guide whether these files should be held, analyzed, or published.

AAFC Crop Inventory

The coloured raster is Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada’s satellite-derived crop and land-cover classification. Use the legend for class colours and the Map Layers switches for 2011 or 2024. The web display is reprojected, clipped to official provincial boundaries, and downsampled; nearest-neighbour processing preserves class codes.

Important limitations

This map provides research and geographic context. It is not a legal survey or title system. It does not yet contain complete province-wide owners, buyers, sellers, prices, mortgages, or historical transfers. Public Indigenous layers do not define the full extent of Indigenous territories or jurisdiction.
